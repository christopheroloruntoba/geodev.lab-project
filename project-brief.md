# My Project Brief

## The Question
Which settlements in Ibadan North sit in low-lying land near watercourses?

## Why It Matters
Identifying settlements in low-lying areas close to watercourses can help local authorities identify locations that may be more vulnerable to flooding. The results can support flood-risk assessment, drainage planning, and prioritization of areas for further investigation.

## The Data I Need
LGA/ward boundaries
Settlement locations or settlement extents
Watercourses/rivers/streams
Digital Elevation Model (DEM)
Possibly roads, if accessibility or emergency response is relevant

## Where Each Dataset Comes From
- Ward boundaries – GRID3 – https://data.grid3.org – GeoPackage
- Settlement extents – GRID3 – https://data.grid3.org – GeoPackage 
- Watercourses – OSM via QuickOSM – extracted for my LGA
- Elevation – Copernicus DEM 30 m – https://portal.opentopography.org

## What You Would Build
I would build a GIS map showing settlements located in low-lying areas in Ibadan North LGA. The map would highlight potentially flood-prone settlements and provide a summary table that can support local flood-risk planning.
